<Update label="July 31, 2026" tags={["New","Improvement"]}>
  **New Account**

  **Tap & Go Account :** A new account type is now available for Tap & Go merchants. The **Tap & Go Account** allows you to accept payments directly from your customers via QR Code, USSD, or NFC, without requiring an internet connection (for USSD payments) or a physical POS terminal. Thanks to the Tap & Go mobile app, merchants can now track all transactions made		by their customers in one place.

  Account creation requires submitting information and documents as part of FedaPay’s general KYC process, including the legal representative's **CIP**, the **IFU**, and optionally the **RCCM or company registration number**.

  [Learn more](tap-go/en/faq-en)
</Update>

<Update label="July 31, 2026" tags={["Improvement"]}>
  **Account Validation & Risk Management Improvements**

  **Context :** Following an increase in fraudulent transaction reports and to align with regulatory requirements under our license, the account validation process has been revised and improved.

  - **Introduction of new documents: The Sworn Statement, General Terms of Use (CGU), and Service Level Agreement (SLA)** must now be downloaded, completed, and uploaded during the validation process.
  - **Removal of fields :** The _signature_ and _Capital_ fields have been removed.
</Update>

<Update label="February 5, 2026" tags={["New"]}>
  - **TAP & GO Available on iOS :** The TAP & GO app is now available on iOS. Merchants can download it from the App Store and accept payments directly on their iPhone.
  - **TAP & GO V2 :** In this new version of the app, merchants can now initiate a payment directly via QR Code or USSD for their customers without needing physical support. This update makes payment collection more autonomous, fast, and flexible.
</Update>

<Update label="January 24, 2026" tags={["Improvement"]}>
  <span style={{color: "#008029"}}>Description</span> **Field Added for Payouts :** An optional <span style={{color: "#008029"}}>description</span> field is now available when creating a payout. It allows merchants to specify the context or purpose of the deposit, improve transaction tracking (Dashboard & API), and simplify regulatory compliance-particularly regarding BCEAO requirements.
</Update>

<Update label="November 7, 2025" tags={["New","Improvement"]}>
